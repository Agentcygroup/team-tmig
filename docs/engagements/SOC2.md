# SOC 2 Type II Engagement

Type: attestation engagement
Standard: AICPA Trust Services Criteria (TSC 2017, revised 2022)
Report: SOC 2 Type II opinion letter
Deliverable: audit report covering a 3–12 month observation window
Prerequisites: ISMS, controls documented, evidence collected, policies
Provider types: licensed CPA firm, must be AICPA member
Typical firms: Deloitte, EY, KPMG, PwC, Grant Thornton, RSM, BDO, Moss Adams, Sensiba, Prescient Assurance, Johanson Group, Linford & Co
Scope options: Security, Availability, Processing Integrity, Confidentiality, Privacy
Observation window: minimum 3 months, typical 6-12 months
Cost range: $25k (small firm, Security only, 3mo) to $250k+ (Big 4, all criteria, 12mo)
Duration: 4 to 15 months from kickoff to report
Reciprocity: US-centric, recognized by enterprises worldwide
Output artifacts: SOC 2 Type II report PDF, bridge letter, management assertion
Public release: usually under NDA, sometimes public
Expiry: annual re-engagement required
