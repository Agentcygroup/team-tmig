# FedRAMP Authorization

Type: government authorization
Standard: NIST SP 800-53 rev 5, FedRAMP baselines (Low, Moderate, High)
Report: Authority to Operate (ATO)
Provider: 3PAO (Third-Party Assessment Organization) + JAB or agency sponsor
Typical 3PAOs: Coalfire, Schellman, Kratos, SecureIT, Veris
Cost: $250k (Low) to $2M+ (High)
Duration: 6 to 24 months
