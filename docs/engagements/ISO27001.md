# ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Certification Engagement

Type: certification
Standard: ISO/IEC 27001:2022, ISO/IEC 27002:2022
Report: certificate issued by accredited certification body
Deliverable: ISO 27001 certificate + Statement of Applicability audit
Prerequisites: ISMS, risk assessment, controls implemented, internal audit, management review
Provider types: accredited certification body (CB), accredited by a national accreditation body (AB)
Typical firms: BSI, DNV, TÜV, Bureau Veritas, LRQA, SGS, Intertek, A-LIGN, Schellman
Accreditation chain: IAF → national AB (ANAB US, UKAS UK, DAkkS DE, JAB JP, CNAS CN) → CB → auditor
Stages: Stage 1 (documentation review) → Stage 2 (implementation audit) → certificate
Surveillance: annual surveillance audits for 2 years, recertification in year 3
Cost range: $15k (small scope) to $150k+ (large scope, multi-site)
Duration: 3 to 12 months from kickoff to certificate
Reciprocity: global, mutual recognition via IAF MLA
Output artifacts: certificate, Statement of Applicability, audit report, nonconformity log
Public release: certificate number public, audit report confidential
Expiry: 3 years, with annual surveillance
