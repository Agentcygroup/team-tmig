# Public Corpus Retrieval

Sources enumerated by authority type: FOIA reading rooms, open data portals,
official statistical agencies, public registries, peer-reviewed repositories,
and international standards bodies. Every source is public and reachable.

## Authority classes

  C1  US federal FOIA reading rooms
  C2  US state open data
  C3  International statistical agencies
  C4  International standards bodies (public catalogues)
  C5  Peer-reviewed research repositories
  C6  Intergovernmental open data
  C7  Non-governmental transparency portals
  C8  Municipal and county registries
